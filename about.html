Why this exists

A website for jewellers who never had one.

Most family jewellers in Bhopal still sell from the counter: New Market, 10 Number, Kolar, Itwara. Clients already trust them. What they lack is a catalogue that can be shared at 11 at night, with making and GST written the way the bill will look.

Rivaayat is that catalogue. The shop keeps the gold. The client talks to the shop. We only host the storefront — four ready designs — and the calculator.

How a price is built

Metal value = weight × today’s karat rate
Wastage = metal value × shop wastage %
Making = (metal + wastage) × shop making %
GST = 3% on metal (incl. wastage) + 5% on making
Hallmark = ₹45 per piece

Rates on the banner are indicative for Bhopal on the date shown. The jeweller’s invoice is the last word.
